# Brand artwork sources

These files are unchanged copies of retained Sage Advice brand-kit assets. The manifest records their SHA-256 hashes and source handoff identifiers.

## Paired sage leaves

`sage-leaves-daytime.png` and `sage-leaves-after-hours.png` are raster derivatives of the approved paired-leaf direction.

Hanna requested the standalone profile icons, then tighter daytime framing. On October 6, 2026, she requested inclusion of both in the brand kit.

The daytime version is the current profile choice. The dark alternate retains the earlier framing. Both source files remain unchanged here.

The source handoff is `brand-kit/sage-leaf-icons/manifest.json`. Its design reference is the round-05 Sage Advice board.

## Bowerloom wordmark

The ink and cream wordmarks use the approved round-07 plain composition. All three o letters are ordinary.

These SVG files embed raster artwork. They are not the separate vector reconstruction candidate.

Hanna accepted the delivered landing with these wordmarks on October 5, 2026. This profile preserves their file bytes, colors, and proportions.

## S4-G3

The color and dark-background icons come from round-05. The dark variant keeps the ears visible against a dark page.

S4-G3 is an illustrated mascot. Its appearance does not establish an employee identity or a software capability.
